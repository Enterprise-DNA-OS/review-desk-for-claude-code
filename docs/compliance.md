# Review integrity and privacy checks

This desk records evidence for a human review. It cannot determine whether a reviewer actually used a service, inspect private account evidence automatically or certify legal compliance. A checked assessment means an operator recorded their checks. It is not an authenticity guarantee.

## Rules and sources

| Rule | Source | What the command checks |
|---|---|---|
| POLICY | [ACCC online reviews guidance](https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/advertising-and-promotions/online-reviews-for-product-and-services) | Missing publication policy reference or overdue policy review |
| REVIEW-CHECK | [NZ Commerce Commission online reviews guidance](https://www.comcom.govt.nz/business/dealing-with-typical-situations/ensuring-customers-get-a-complete-picture-of-your-online-reviews/) | Missing source assessment for the current version |
| REVIEW-CONCERN | ACCC guidance above | Uncertain or concern assessment requires human investigation |
| DISCLOSURE | ACCC guidance above | Relationship or incentive recorded as undisclosed or unknown |
| PRIVACY-5 | [NZ privacy principle 5](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/5/) | Missing recorded access-safeguards evidence |
| PRIVACY-9 | [NZ privacy principle 9](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/9/) | A location’s chosen retention review date has passed |

The ACCC describes risks from fake reviews, undisclosed relationships or incentives and suppressing genuine negative reviews. Incentives must not depend on a positive rating and need disclosure. The software sends no invitations, makes no moderation changes and includes every imported rating in reports. An operator can record an uncertain case and service action without asserting that the review is fake.

The NZ guidance requires genuine reviews presented without a misleading change in meaning. Source versions are append only, so edited source text never overwrites the old record. Drafts are business replies, never fabricated customer reviews.

Privacy principle 5 concerns reasonable safeguards. A nonempty evidence field does not prove those safeguards exist. Principle 9 concerns keeping information only while needed for a lawful purpose. The review date is the owner’s chosen control, not a statutory retention period. This base flags retention review but does not delete records; the owner must arrange controlled removal or anonymisation across backups, exports, drafts and logs when required.

## Internal controls, not legal deadlines

The default three-day response target and seven-day freshness check are internal working rules. They are not legal deadlines. A separate named reviewer, exact-text approval and a source-version check are local workflow controls. Supplied names are not authenticated signatures. A no-response decision preserves the review and the reason, and never removes it from a platform.

Before approving, read the actual reply for personal information, unsupported promises and factual accuracy. Approval requires privacy confirmation, current policy/access/retention records and a checked disclosure assessment. Later source changes or new drafts make old approvals unusable. Record-posting records an operator’s manual posting evidence and sends nothing.

All sources checked 10 November 2026. Local legal requirements and platform rules still need the operator’s professional review.
