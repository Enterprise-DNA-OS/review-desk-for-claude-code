# Selection and research: Grade.us

Checked 10 November 2026. Family: online-review-operations. The registry has feedback theme analysis (Thematic), social publishing, general complaints and recurring procedure execution, but no dedicated location review response desk with source versions, exact reply approval and manual posting evidence.

US$21,600 a year is a sizing scenario for 50 locations at the published US$36 monthly per-location rate in the 41-100 range. It is not a customer invoice or an Agency-plan quote. The same range lists US$29 monthly equivalent with annual billing. The Agency card asks for a quote; do not confuse the site’s older agency calculator with a current quote.

Scores, each out of five: annual bill 3; data-shaped 5; buyer fit 2; search demand 3; one-session buildable 5; import path 4. Total 22/30. No EDNA customer adoption or invoice was verified. Local customer records show interest in displaying online reviews, not ownership of Grade.us. No live CRM invoice was verified. Search interest is qualitative, not measured volume.

GatherUp scored 1/5/2/3/5/4 = 20 using its published 2-10 location pricing; larger portfolios require a quote. AppFollow scored 1/5/2/2/5/4 = 19 with no five-figure price verified from the current pricing page. Suralink was rejected because no public five-figure bill was verified. Tools with a network moat and tools from already-built families were excluded.
